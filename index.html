Expertise

The class I and class II antigen processing and presentation pathways are the arbiters of what T-cells can or can't target. This sensitive biological system can be tissue specific and is of critical importance to immunotherapy efficacy and resistance. My support turns antigen presentation biology from an obstacle into a resource that moves your project forward!

Understanding cancer requires understanding tissue-specific biology, fundamentals of cell proliferation and differentiation, as well as evolution through natural selection. Positive clinical outcomes build on complex concepts such as the cancer immunity cycle, immunoediting, and defining promising disease-specific targets based on the available data. With my support complex cancer biology becomes accessible, sharpening your toolset to fight the disease!

Genomics, transcriptomics, immunopeptidomics, proteomics, shmoteomics — contemporary tools are ample and complex. The difficult part is to know where to apply them and make them fall in line. With my support we will extract knowledge from a concert of sources to build harmony that reflects a true observation!
