Expertise

The class I and class II antigen processing and presentation pathways are the arbiters of what T-cells can or can't target. This sensitive biological system needs to be understood in a tissue-specific context and is of critical importance to immunotherapy efficacy and resistance. My support turns antigen presentation biology from an obstacle into a resource that moves your project forward!

Understanding cancer requires understanding tissue-specific biology, fundamentals of cell proliferation and differentiation, as well as evolution through natural selection. Positive clinical outcomes build on complex concepts such as the cancer immunity cycle, immunoediting, and defining promising disease-specific targets based on the available data. With my support complex cancer biology becomes accessible, sharpening your toolset to fight the disease!

Genomics, transcriptomics, immunopeptidomics, proteomics, shmoteomics — contemporary tools are ample and complex. The difficult part is to know where to apply them and make them fall in line. With my support we will organize workflows and extract complementary evidence in your multi-omics setting!

Immunopeptidomics is the king of exploring and validating the landscape of HLA-peptides presented by the antigen processing and presentation pathways. Clinical context makes everything harder: personalization, short turnaround times, scarce samples, an obligation towards patients and their families to maximize data extraction, and an exceptionally high confidence thresholds. With my support we can enhance the compatibility of your plans with clinical pipelines and maximize translational value!

The world of LC-MS has a high entry barrier, evolves quickly, and can feel inaccessible to non-experts. Understanding how proteomics data is generated and what it means is truly rewarding. Proteoforms, translons, PTMs — it opens the door to the very heart of biology! With my support you receive expert guidance to crystallize the strongest evidence that your data can provide!

About me

Born and raised in Switzerland, I received my BSc and MSc education at the University of Zurich where I was first exposed to mass spectrometry methods. There, I worked on understanding the pharmacokinetics of new psychoactive substances in the Department of Forensic Toxicology with help of MALDI-MS and LC-MS. The world of mass spectrometry was captivating enough to move into industry and join Biognosys in building their immunopeptidomics capabilities. The end-to-end workflows I built were a great introduction into the field and relied heavily on building pragmatic technical know-how for mass spectrometry hard- and software. Immunopeptidomics turned from a task to a burning interest. To refine my expertise and expand it to clinical applications I joined Prof. Michal Bassani-Sternberg's lab at the University of Lausanne to obtain my PhD in Cancer and Immunology. My work centered around the challenges that antigen prioritization pipelines face and where immunopeptidomics can maximize its impact in these complex proteogenomics pipelines to select immunogenic, cancer-specific targets — and, if necessary, from NGS data only. This demanded a deep understanding cancer immunology, antigen processing biology, immunogenicity screening, and practical conditions and challenges that target discovery pipelines face. I then joined Parallel Squared Technology Institute in Boston to support their development of next-generation mass spectrometry technologies as an expert for cancer biology, immunotherapy, proteogenomics, and immunopeptidomics method development.
